FAQ

Questions before you book

What are your hours? +

We open at 8:00 AM, Monday through Friday. For weekends, call or text and ask.

What's the fastest way to reach you? +

Call or text (504) 494-4485. One message is all it takes to get on the schedule.

My garbage disposal stopped. Anything I can try first? +

Often, yes. Switch it off, then press the red reset button on the bottom of the unit. If it hums but won't turn, the flywheel is jammed: with the power off, turn it by hand using the hex key in the socket underneath. Never put your hand in the disposal. If that doesn't do it, message us.

What kind of work do you take on? +

Garbage disposal repair and replacement, under-sink plumbing, interior painting, and household repairs — lamps, small appliances, fixtures and the rest of the to-do list. Ask about anything that isn't listed.

Do you work on rental properties? +

Yes. Landlords use us for make-readies between tenants, from repainting a whole unit to working through a list of repairs.

Do you cover my area? +

We're based in New Orleans and work across the city and nearby parishes. Check the service area list above, or call and ask if yours isn't on it.
