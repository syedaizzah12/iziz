**3 worst problems** (ranked by damage to the film, each with timestamp + cause)
1. **9:16 is a crop, and both formats leave large dead zones.** 16–19 s: the 9:16 workspace table bleeds off the right edge, so the "Real gain" / "Within noise" verdict, the film's proof point, is cut by the frame edge and sits in the right UI zone. 13–16 s: the prompts card touches the edge. Every 9:16 shot leaves the bottom ~40 % empty. 16:9 type shots (0–2, 4–6, 24–28 s) park small type top-left with ~60 % of the frame empty. Cause: the 9:16 layout reuses the 16:9 card widths instead of re-blocking them, and the hero, number, tagline and lockup are positioned at fixed top-left offsets instead of filling the frame.
2. **The payoff is illegible on a phone.** 28–30 s: the 16:9 CTA label is ~8 px tall at 360 (43 px at 1920) and is the smallest type on the end card, while the wordmark is 37 px. 16–19 s: the verdict pills are ~4 px (16:9) and ~9 px (9:16) at 360, so "real gain vs within noise", a must-show item, never reads at phone size. Cause: CTA set at UI-button size and never scaled for the end card, and no punch-in on the verdict row large enough to bring the pill text to phone size.
3. **At every hit the sound lands before anything you can read.** Captions and content arrive 8–13 frames after their hits: 8.0 (card with "—" and an empty track until f246, caption readable ~8.45), 10.0 / 12.0 / 16.0 / 20.0 (captions are clipped glyphs on the cut frame and still at the quarter-beat: sheet b20, b24, b32, b40), 12.0–12.5 (blank card), 22.0 (caption readable ~22.3, f669), 26.0 (ring without dot, wordmark clipped at 26.1). Seven picture-tied ticks are also 100–167 ms off. Cause: the caption and wordmark springs start on the hit instead of being mostly landed by it, and the card contents and the logo dot are scheduled half a beat late.
