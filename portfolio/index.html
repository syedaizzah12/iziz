Lumen

Finance · Banking · iOS
Glassmorphism

A current account app where cards, balances and sheets are frosted panes over a slow mesh gradient. The glass is used the way iOS uses materials: for surfaces that float above content, never for the content itself.

  • Home, card controls and a send-money sheet
  • Backdrop blur 24px with a one-pixel top highlight
  • Gradient mesh drifts so the glass reads as glass

How a screen gets made.

Every app starts as a flow, not a mood board. The visual language comes last, once the screens already work in grey boxes on a real device.

01Flows and contentWho opens the app, what they came for, what they type.
02Grey-box prototypeTested on a phone at arm's length before any color is picked.
03System and typeOne tint, one type scale, native components wherever they exist.
04Motion and materialGlass, blur, parallax or depth, only where it explains the interface.
05HandoffFigma with variables, a SwiftUI reference build and a motion spec.
