01 · Glassmorphism

Lumen Personal finance that feels lit from behind

Balances, budgets and transactions sit on frosted panes over a slow-moving color field. The glass picks up whatever drifts beneath it, so the interface never looks the same twice.

  • Layered backdrop blur with a one-pixel top highlight for the edge catch
  • Color field reacts to the cursor, so panes refract as you move
  • Numbers stay solid white at 4.5:1 contrast on every background state

02 · Blur

Reverb A music player with depth of field

The artwork is the interface. A heavily blurred copy of the cover becomes the room the song plays in, and a progressive blur at the base lets controls float without a panel. Hover an album to pull it into focus and watch the rest fall away.

  • Progressive blur built from stacked, masked backdrop layers
  • Focus and defocus is the navigation model: what is sharp is what is active
  • Cover-derived color means every album ships its own theme

03 · Parallax

Atlas Trip planning with a sense of distance

Sky, ridges and clouds live on separate planes and move at their own speeds as you scroll. The itinerary card rides the nearest plane, which keeps it readable while the world shifts behind it.

  • Five depth planes driven by scroll position and pointer, no scroll jank
  • Content plane moves least, so copy stays anchored for reading
  • Switches to a flat layout under reduced motion

04 · 3D

Forma Furniture shopping with real depth

The product detail page is a stack of planes in true perspective. Move across it and the lamp, its tags and the buy bar each sit at a different height, so the screen reads like an object rather than a picture of one.

  • Pointer-driven tilt on the device, plus per-element z-offsets inside the screen
  • Specular highlight on the product follows the tilt angle
  • Augmented-reality handoff lives one tap from the buy button

05 · Modern art for kids

Splat A Bauhaus paint box for ages 4 to 9

Big shapes, five loud colors and a brush that never runs out. Every tap of Remix composes a new abstract piece from circles, arcs and triangles, then hands the canvas over so a child can paint on top of it. Try it on the screen.

  • Touch targets of 44 px and over, three-pixel outlines, no small text anywhere
  • Stamps and brush strokes use the same seeded generator, so a piece can be replayed
  • No accounts, no ads, no timers; the only reward is the picture
